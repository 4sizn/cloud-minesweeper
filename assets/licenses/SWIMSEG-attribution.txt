Original data are not included in the production app. The bundled CloudNet
weights were trained locally on deduplicated SWIMSEG photos with capture dates
held apart for validation and evaluation. Images were resized and augmented by
flips and mild exposure changes. This noncommercial development model is not
presented as commercially licensed. No endorsement by the dataset authors is implied.
