The photographs come from the COCO dataset (https://cocodataset.org) and are
Flickr images with per-image licenses. Only images whose COCO license permits
commercial use and derivative works were used for training:
Creative Commons Attribution 2.0, Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.0,
images with no known copyright restrictions, and United States Government works.
Images licensed NonCommercial or NoDerivatives were excluded.

No photographs are included in this app. The bundled CloudNet weights were
trained locally on sky-dominated crops of those images, using the COCO-Stuff
"clouds" and "sky-other" annotations, with images held apart for validation and
evaluation. Training used flips and mild exposure changes. No endorsement by the
dataset authors is implied.
